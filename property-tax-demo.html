[Before You Book]

Common Questions

Do I have to change the tools I already use?+
No. The system replaces the spreadsheet and the mental tracking, not your email or your filing process with the appraisal district. You keep working the way you work; the system keeps the record.
My fee structure is different. Will the invoice math still work?+
The calculator above is built around one consultant's real rates and client types. Yours would be built around your own fee structure during the engagement, and tested against your past cases before it goes live.
How long does setup take?+
A scoped single-module build delivers in about 14 days. The full case manager and invoice engine together typically takes a few weeks, and you test it against your own past cases before anything goes live.
Is my client data secure?+
Yes. Each account sees only its own cases, enforced at the database level, and your invoice records stay in your own system. Nothing is shared across clients or practices.
