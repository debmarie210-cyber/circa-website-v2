# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

SMB founders and operators in service-heavy industries — insurance, financial services, professional services. They're running real businesses with real revenue (typically $500K–$5M ARR), not experimenting. Their context: overloaded, skeptical of tech hype, burned by consultants who delivered decks instead of results. Primary job: stop revenue from leaking through slow follow-up, manual processes, and gaps they can't see. Secondary job: trust that the person they're hiring actually understands their business, not just AI.

## Product Purpose

Circa AI Group is an AI consulting firm (founded by Debbie Wright) that identifies where AI will save a service business the most time and money — then builds it. Not strategy-only. Not a SaaS tool. A done-with-you implementation practice.

The marketing site exists to: (1) qualify and convert inbound leads into booked AI strategy calls, and (2) establish Debbie's authority as a practitioner, not a theorist. Success = booked calls and referrals from clients who felt seen, not sold to.

A product surface (app/dashboard) is planned. The current brand system must scale to serve both.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, credible, calm.

Expert confidence without the noise. The site should feel like a boutique firm that has seen enough bad AI implementations to know exactly what works — and doesn't need to prove it loudly. Quiet authority over performed enthusiasm.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS / Webflow templates** — blue gradients, stock photography, "scale your business" hero copy, endless feature grids. The aesthetic that says "I downloaded a theme."
- Nothing that looks like it was generated from a template, chosen from a library, or designed to appeal to everyone.

## Design Principles

1. **Problem before solution** — Every section leads with the client's pain, not Circa's capabilities. The empathy earns the pitch.
2. **Numbers do the talking** — Credibility is quantified. Revenue figures, timelines, and outcomes replace adjectives wherever possible.
3. **Restraint is authority** — One CTA per section. One gold element per section. Whitespace is confidence. Busyness signals insecurity.
4. **Earned luxury** — Navy and gold aren't decoration; they're a claim. Every design choice must justify that positioning or be cut.
5. **System scales up** — Design decisions made for the brand site must hold when a product surface arrives. No one-off tricks that break at app complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. High-contrast text on all backgrounds (never gold on cream). Keyboard-navigable CTAs and modals. Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Screen reader-friendly structure (semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, alt text on all images).
